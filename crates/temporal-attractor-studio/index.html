📚 About Lyapunov Exponents

The Lyapunov exponent measures how fast nearby trajectories diverge in a dynamical system. It's the key to understanding chaos and predictability:

  • λ > 0: System is chaotic (butterfly effect)
  • λ ≈ 0: System is periodic or quasiperiodic
  • λ < 0: System converges to stable fixed point

The Lyapunov time (1/λ) tells you how long predictions remain useful. After this time, small errors have grown exponentially large.
